For businesses

Check that a credential really came from Ibex

A customer has shown you a score. This page tells you whether Ibex issued it, whether it has been altered since, and whether it is bound to the name their bank holds. No account needed.

Verify by customer details

Enter the details exactly as the customer gave them. Ibex recomputes the pseudonymous reference from the name their bank returned and reads the matching record from the contract.

Verify by score file

If the customer exported score-event.json, paste it here. This is the strongest check: the whole record is hashed and compared with the anchored hash.

Verify by reference

The customer can share their pseudonymous reference without revealing anything else. It looks like 0x followed by 64 characters.

What a match proves

  • Ibex issued this score, and the figure has not been edited since it was anchored.
  • It is bound to the name the customer's bank returned over open banking, not a name they typed in.
  • You can see when it was issued and which model version produced it.

What it does not prove

  • That the person in front of you is the subject of the credential. That needs a signed wallet challenge, which Ibex does not yet issue.
  • That the customer is creditworthy today. A score is a point-in-time estimate, not a lending decision.
  • Anything about their finances. Only hashes reach the chain.
Ibex Credit Verification is pull-based and consent-first.
