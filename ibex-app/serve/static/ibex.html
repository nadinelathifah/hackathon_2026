We request 24 months of transaction history. Your bank authenticates you directly — Ibex never sees your credentials.

Before you connect (optional, ~20 seconds)

Three declarations improve your score's accuracy. Declared income is cross-checked against what your bank shows. Prefer not to say is fine — unanswered fields are treated as missing, never as a fabricated default.

We ask nothing about education or housing — education was removed as a fairness precaution, and housing added no predictive power once your bank history is known.

This takes 30–60 seconds. Five accounts, roughly 18,000 transactions, fetched in 180-day chunks because that is the provider limit.
